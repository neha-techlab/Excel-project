SUPERMARKET SALES ANALYSIS

DATASET DESCRIPTION

The Supermarket Sales dataset contains transaction-level sales information from a supermarket. It includes details such as date, branch, customer type, gender, product line, unit price, quantity, total sales, payment method, and customer rating.Slicers provide interactive filters to view sales based on branch, customer type, gender, product line, and month.Users can apply different filters to compare branches, product lines, customer types, genders, and months. Overall, the dashboard helps transform raw sales data into meaningful insights for better understanding of business performance.The timeline displays sales data across the year 2024, helping to analyze and compare month-wise sales trends.The dashboard analyzes six product lines based on their total sales, quantity sold, and average customer rating.It shows the contribution of Cash, Credit Card, and E-wallet to total sales.

KEY FINDINGS

Brooklyn branch generated the highest total sales.

Food & Beverages had the highest quantity sold among all product    lines.

E-wallet was the leading payment method by total sales.

Normal customers generated higher total sales than Member customers.

Female customers generated higher total sales than Male customers.

Food & Beverages had the highest average customer rating. 

Manhattan  branch recorded the highest average rating.
